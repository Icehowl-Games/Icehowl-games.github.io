Drive to dodge. Stop to fire.

A top-down tank roguelike for Android. Break through the Iron Fleet across a five-stage campaign, take an upgrade after every wave, and fight your way into the Citadel.

Coming soon to Google PlayFree to download. One purchase unlocks the full game.

The game

  • A five-stage campaignEvery run draws its route from ten battlefields, from the Verdant Front to Deadwater Delta, each with a mechanic of its own. Every road ends at the Citadel.
  • 14 bossesGOLIATH, GEDDON, THE DREADNOUGHT, THE LEVIATHAN and more, with parts to shoot off and weak points to find. The last is DOMINATOR.
  • Over 100 upgradesFour rarities, evolutions and synergies, plus Contraband from the Boneyard's black market: real power, with a real drawback.
  • Six vehiclesFrom the Ironclad main battle tank to the super-heavy Mammoth. Each one changes a rule.
  • Operation ReclamationLoop the campaign with everything you've built, one Escalation higher each time.
  • Weekly operationThe same seed, vehicle and Escalation for every player, ranked on an online leaderboard.
