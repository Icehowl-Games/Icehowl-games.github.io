More to fight for

  • The CitadelHack through the Iron Fleet's mountain fortress floor by floor. Face DOMINATOR across four phases and escape as its core melts down.
  • Equipment cratesGrab crates on the battlefield to call in fire missions, strafing runs and supply drops.
  • The BoneyardTrack down a salvage beacon to reach a black market. Its experimental tech has real power and a real catch.
  • EscalationRaise the Escalation for harder runs and a higher score. Operation Reclamation loops the campaign with everything you've built.
  • Weekly operationsEvery player gets the same run each week, ranked on an online leaderboard.
  • Ranks and medalsRise from Private to General and unlock paint schemes for every vehicle. The Codex records every enemy, boss and upgrade you come across.
