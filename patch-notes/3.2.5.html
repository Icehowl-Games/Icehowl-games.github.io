Patch Notes 3.2.5

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 27 November 2026

Highlights

  • Boss charges are marked by chevron lanes on the ground.
  • MONOLITH's ammunition core opens behind sliding blast doors.
  • DOMINATOR's dome keeps its shape as you drive round it.

Charge warnings

  • A boss about to charge marks its path with a lane of chevrons. It used to be a soft red line.
  • The lane is painted on the ground under your tank. The old line was drawn over it.
  • Once a charge is set its chevrons run faster and turn white-hot.
  • REAPER's strafing runs are marked with the same lane.
  • THE LEVIATHAN's torpedo runs are marked with a blue lane.
  • GEDDON's lane is as wide as GEDDON. It used to be a thin line.

MONOLITH

  • The ammunition core's housing is shut by two blast doors with MONOLITH's star across them.
  • On a reload the doors slam open to each side and show the shell magazine. It used to show a flat yellow disc.
  • The magazine turns one shell at a time and is lit orange from below.
  • The doors slam shut when the core is sealed.
  • Once the core is destroyed the doors stay jammed part open over a dark pit.

DOMINATOR

  • DOMINATOR's dome no longer swells past its base as you drive along it. Its sides used to bow outward.

Bosses

  • A boss gun that's shot off leaves no mark on the boss. It used to leave a dark disc with an orange ring.
