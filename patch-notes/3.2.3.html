Patch Notes 3.2.3

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 26 November 2026

Highlights

  • Every enemy and boss now winds up before it attacks.
  • IRON HORSE rears and then gallops its charge in a straight line.
  • THE RESEARCHER dodges with a single sideways hop.
  • Soldiers walk with a full stride and raise their weapons to aim.
  • 3D models are lit by the explosions, fires and flashes around them.

Enemies

  • Tanks hold their aim for a moment before they fire. They used to fire as soon as their gun came round.
  • A tank's muzzle glows and its line of fire is drawn out while it aims.
  • IFVs take almost twice as long to settle before an autocannon burst.
  • Riflemen and RPG teams take longer to aim.
  • A glow builds at a soldier's muzzle while he aims.
  • RPGs throw out a backblast.
  • Field engineers stop and raise their carbines before each shot. They used to fire on the move.
  • Flame troopers take half again as long to light up.
  • The cone a flame trooper is about to fill is drawn out first.
  • Flame troopers are shoved back and shaken by their own spray.
  • Mortar crews lift each round over the tube before they drop it in.
  • Recon cars raise a lit flare before they fire it.
  • The spot a recon car's flare will call down is marked on your tank.
  • Flak halftracks swing their guns onto you and settle before they fire.
  • Snow dozers rev for longer before they charge.
  • A snow dozer squats back and shakes while it revs. Its lane is marked out ahead of it.
  • Armoured train cars wind up before each attack.
  • Kamikaze drones pull up and hang for a moment before they dive at you. They used to fly straight in.
  • A kamikaze drone's lamp races and a line reaches out to your tank while it locks on.
  • Kamikaze drones dive faster once they've locked on.

Enemy movement

  • An enemy wedged in a tight spot backs out the way it came in. It used to rock back and forth on the spot.
  • Recon cars circle you on open ground. They used to drive round one spot when their circle crossed rock.
  • A recon car that ends up back where it was takes a new circle the other way.

Recoil

  • Enemy guns are thrown back hard when they fire.
  • Tanks, tank destroyers and train cars rock back with each shot.
  • Soldiers and snipers jolt back with each shot.

Aircraft

  • Helicopters nose down onto you before they fire rockets. They used to fire at once.
  • Helicopters, dive bombers and kamikaze drones bank into their turns.
  • Dive bombers nose down into their dive and pull up out of it.
  • REAPER banks into its turns and noses down before each attack.

Bosses

  • Every heavy boss holds still for a moment as an attack begins. It sinks low and shudders while a ring closes in on it.
  • Boss guns are thrown back hard by each shot.
  • GOLIATH holds its volley longer before the first shell. Its shells come a little further apart.
  • GOLIATH's shell fan is drawn out wider before it fires.
  • GOLIATH revs against its brakes before a charge.
  • BASTION's missile pods and drone hatch glow before they launch.
  • BASTION's railgun charges up at its tip.
  • BASTION shudders as its EMP gathers in pulsing rings.
  • IRON HORSE trots faster.
  • IRON HORSE rears back before it charges. Its line is locked half a second before it goes.
  • IRON HORSE gallops its charge in a straight line at up to 220. It used to walk at 58 and turn after you.
  • IRON HORSE's lane is drawn for the whole length of its charge.
  • THE RESEARCHER dodges with a single sideways hop. It used to slide aside.
  • THE RESEARCHER walks faster.
  • THE RESEARCHER marks where its turrets and security cars will land.
  • THE RESEARCHER's trap charges up bigger and brighter.
  • REAPER takes longer over each wind-up.
  • REAPER's sweep is drawn round from where it starts.
  • JUGGERNAUT's locomotive guns glow before each volley.

Animation

  • Soldiers walk with a full stride at 30 frames a second. They used to switch between two poses.
  • Soldiers bring their weapons up to their shoulder to aim.
  • Soldiers' shoulders swing as they walk.
  • Mortar crews carrying their tube walk out of step.
  • IRON HORSE trots with quicker and higher steps.
  • THE RESEARCHER takes longer and higher strides.
  • The walking bosses' legs move through a third more poses.

Lighting

  • 3D models are lit by the explosions, fires, flashes and glows around them.
