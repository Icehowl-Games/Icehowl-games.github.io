Patch Notes 3.1.1

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 18 November 2026

Highlights

  • Every front has a 3D diorama in the Codex.
  • Each of the ten aces wears its own paint.
  • Bulwark's shield is a dome of hexagonal panes standing over your tank.
  • Enemies find their way round bosses, walls and each other better.
  • The escape from the Citadel has explosions all across the floor and rock falling from the ceiling.

Codex

  • Each front's page opens on a small 3D model of the place: its ground and what stands on it. The Fronts tiles show them too. All thirteen fronts and the Boneyard have one.
  • Text in Codex entries is spaced correctly. Words in some entries were running together.
  • The aces' tiles and pages show them in their new paint.

Aces

  • Each ace has its own colours and markings, on the battlefield and in the Codex:
    • IRON WOLF: steel grey, with claw slashes across its turret.
    • THE SCYTHE: bone white, with a red blade across its deck.
    • NIGHTHAWK: midnight blue.
    • THE GHOST: pale grey.
    • HAMMERFALL: dark iron, with yellow bands.
    • FIREBRAND: charred black, with flame running back from its bow.
    • SNOWBLIND: ice blue.
    • BANSHEE: crimson, with pale bands round its wings.
    • THE MOLE: earth brown, with orange chevrons.
    • LANCER: deep green, with a pale stripe down its middle.

Upgrades

  • Bulwark's shield is a see-through dome of hexagonal panes over the tank. The panes flare where a shot strikes it.
  • Regimental Colors is gone from the Armory. It had already been removed from runs. If you bought it, its REQ is returned.
  • In the Armory, the upgrades every run offers from the start are listed at the top of each section.

On the battlefield

  • Your tank's track marks run directly behind its own tracks or wheels, at their width, for every vehicle.
  • Enemies steer round a boss, and round other units standing in their way, instead of pushing into them. One that finds its way shut takes another route sooner.
  • Sandstorms and snowstorms no longer draw a bar across the bottom of the screen.
  • MIRAGE's wheels are drawn correctly on both sides.

The Citadel

  • On floors 2 and 3, the doorways between the side halls are in the middle of their walls.
  • During the escape, explosions go off all across the floor and loose rock falls from the ceiling in plain sight. The marked explosions come a little faster, and rock falls near the tank more often.

Boot Camp

  • The INSTRUCTOR drives round cover and trees instead of into them.
