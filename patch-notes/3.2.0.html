Patch Notes 3.2.0

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 24 November 2026

Highlights

  • Use items are added: 12 items you activate yourself, found in equipment crates.
  • The Flamethrower is added as a new Legendary gun.
  • Iron Tundra's radar station is replaced by a fortress with a shielded satellite dish.
  • The Service Record is rebuilt into tiles, with a new Run History page.
  • The Chinook and the security turrets are now 3D models.
  • Air and artillery support no longer work indoors.

Use items

  • 12 use items are added. Before, nothing in a run was activated by hand.
  • Equipment items recharge on a cooldown: Damage Control, Overdrive, Starshell, Canister Round, Satchel Charge, Crash Load.
  • Support items recharge with kills: Fire Mission, Strafing Run, Blockbuster, Supply Drop, Wingman, Radio Jamming.
  • Item buttons sit under the LOCK button and show the item's icon.
  • Each vehicle has its own item slots.
  • Equipment is marked orange and Support blue.
  • The Codex has a new EQUIPMENT tab with a page for each item.
  • An item's Codex page opens when you first buy it and fills after you choose it 10 times.

Equipment crates

  • Green equipment crates can now appear on a level. Each holds one use item.
  • Tap a crate to see its offer, then buy it or leave it.
  • Crates are bought with CP. The price starts at 8 CP and goes up 2 each stage.
  • With your slots full, you pick which item to replace.

Indoors

  • Gunships, artillery and naval fire no longer reach you indoors. Before, they worked everywhere.
  • This covers the Citadel below the roof and the Ruinous Facility.
  • Fire Mission, Strafing Run and Blockbuster are disabled indoors and show NO SKY.
  • Supply Drop comes by runner indoors instead of by parachute.
  • The radio's Air Strike and Precision Strike show NO SKY indoors.
  • Enemy mortars, aircraft and recon artillery no longer work indoors either.
  • A message tells you when you go in and when you come back out.

Flamethrower

  • The Flamethrower is added as a Legendary upgrade. It replaces the main gun, like the Railgun and the Avenger Cannon.
  • It sprays a jet of fire to half the gun's range and sets everything in it alight.
  • It does 50% of main gun damage a second.
  • It burns aircraft too.
  • It is unlocked by killing 900 enemies with fire, then bought with REQ in the Armory.
  • It has synergies with Incendiary Rounds, Long Barrel and Crocodile.
  • It is not offered with the Railgun, Avenger Cannon, AP Rounds or Flak Curtain, or to the Sapper and Skyguard.

Iron Tundra

  • The radar station is replaced by a large concrete fortress with a satellite dish beside it.
  • The fortress can't be destroyed. Its garrison fires rifles from the windows and cracks your armor.
  • The dish is under a shield and can't be damaged while it is up.
  • Three power sources now stand around the map, in different places each run, with cables running to the dish.
  • Destroying all three lowers the shield. Then the dish can be destroyed.
  • The dish keeps the radar station's effect: while it stands, snowstorms don't hide you.

Ruinous Facility

  • The rooms' walls are now lined with specimen tanks, rock drills, crated artefacts, control desks and notes boards. Before, the rooms were mostly bare.
  • The wall lights are now caged work lamps mounted on the walls. Before, they were white circles on the floor.
  • Lamps are only placed on walls.
  • The red-roofed building in the vehicle hangar is removed.

Models and art

  • The Chinook is now a 3D model with a rotor turning over each mast. Before, it was a flat picture.
  • Security turrets are now 3D models with a base, a turning gun housing and twin barrels. Before, they were a grey disc with a blue dot.
  • Strafing Run's fighter is a 3D model that banks in and climbs away.
  • Tank names, numbers, insignia and turret rings are now painted on the tank's model. Before, they hovered over it.
  • The insignia now takes the place of the turret's star.
  • The hull number now reads from the front.
  • Radio whips and the pennant are now part of the model.
  • The Vehicles screen shows the same markings on its turntables.

Service Record

  • The Service Record is rebuilt into five tiles: Tutorials, Feats, Medals, Codex and Run History. Before, it was a set of long tabs.
  • Tutorials now has its own page. Before, it was a tab in the Codex.
  • Run History is added, with your best run for each vehicle first and your recent runs after.
  • The Boot Camp ribbon now shows on the Boot Camp button. Before, it was in Medals.

Codex

  • Upgrades are now grouped under headings: Basic, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Evolutions and Contraband.
  • Contraband now has its own section. Before, it was mixed in with the others.

Escalation and leaderboard

  • Each Escalation level now adds 3% to your score. Before, Escalation paid nothing.
  • The Escalation picker now shows the score bonus for the level you pick.
  • Leaderboard rows no longer show weather and night symbols.

Radio and store

  • The radio's Supply Drop order is renamed Supply Order.
  • The full game window's text is shortened so it no longer gets cut off.
  • If the store can't offer the full game, it now says why. Before, it only said the store wasn't ready.
