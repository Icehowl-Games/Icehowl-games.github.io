Patch Notes 3.1.3

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 18 November 2026

Highlights

  • Level unlocks are back: each stage starts with one level open, and beating a boss opens the next.
  • The railyard's roundhouse is one large building around the turntable, and every track end has a buffer stop.
  • Iron Tundra has a radar station to destroy, and Altstadt's church and station now look like a church and a station.
  • The foundry and the railyard are dressed with the tools and stores of the people who work there.
  • Boot Camp shows you each objective before you do it, and its music no longer cuts out.

Level unlocks

  • Each stage begins with one level open: Verdant Front, Altstadt, Iron Tundra, Red Basin and the Citadel.
  • Beating the boss on a level opens the next level of that stage: Hollow Fields after Verdant Front; Black Coast after Altstadt; Steel Corridor, then Deadwater Delta, after Iron Tundra; Ashen Foundry, then Broken Crown, after Red Basin.
  • Everyone starts from the opening five with this update.
  • The weekly run uses every level, as before.
  • A newly opened level is announced when the boss falls, and each level's Codex page shows whether it is open.
  • Ashen Foundry is now a Stage 4 level and Deadwater Delta a Stage 3 level.

Steel Corridor

  • The roundhouse is one C-shaped building around the turntable. Every stall's track runs in at its own doors, some shut and some open on an engine inside.
  • Every open track end has a buffer stop.
  • Spare wheelsets and bogies, oil racks, signal levers, luggage trolleys, parcels and benches are set out around the yard, the shops, the goods depot and the station forecourt.

Ashen Foundry

  • Mould bogies, slag pots, tool racks, pig iron, workbenches, gas cylinders, plate stacks and scrap skips are set out in rows where they are used.
  • Overhead beams, cranes and catwalks stand on posts at each end.
  • Its sidings run out of the works through gated openings.
  • The marked roads outside the playable area are gone. The paving between the works' lots is as wide as the game's own roads.
  • Rails now show on its paved floors.

Iron Tundra

  • A radar station stands on the snowfield. While it stands, a snowstorm no longer hides you from distant enemies. Destroy it and the storms hide you again; it drops three pickups.
  • Its sidings leave the main line at proper turnouts.
  • Tracks show over ice, mud and water patches.

Altstadt

  • The church is a church: a slate nave with a transept, a tower with a spire and cross, and a churchyard. Its building was being cleared away by mistake, which left only a statue.
  • The station has a station building with a clock turret and a canopy over the platform.
  • The three landmarks stand further apart across the town.
  • Buildings, walls and stores no longer stand on its roads. The same is fixed on Black Coast.

Ruinous Facility

  • The floor is level everywhere except the great hall's gallery, which is a raised strip with an even slope around it.
  • Its siding runs on into a tunnel.

Every level

  • Walls and blocks meet the floor at a clean right angle. The floor beside them was drawn as a short slope with its corners cut off.

Boot Camp

  • When a radio call asks for something out of view, the camera goes over to it, holds, and comes back.
  • The music no longer cuts out. The same fault is fixed for level tracks that loop from late in the piece.
  • In the wave lesson, the enemy appears once you reach the next clearing, not before.
  • An upgrade card's evolution icon is in a square box again.
  • The terminal lesson says that the Boneyard's briefcases work the same way as the Citadel's terminals.

Tablets and large screens

  • The game is no longer forced upright on tablets, foldables and desktop windows. In a wide window it keeps an upright shape in the middle. Phones stay upright.
