Patch Notes 3.1.2

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 18 November 2026

Highlights

  • New fire and explosions across the whole game.
  • DOMINATOR comes apart in a death sequence before its core is exposed.
  • The Citadel's upper floors are furnished, and everything in them can be destroyed.
  • Radiation shows how many stacks an enemy carries, and Fallout Tracks leaves a trail of glowing green liquid.
  • Three new pieces of music, and new sounds for explosions, missiles and the Snow Dozer.

Fire and explosions

  • Every explosion is redrawn. Small blasts, up to the size of a cluster bomb, are a quick burst. Anything bigger is a rolling fireball that boils up and turns to smoke.
  • Smoke is drawn as billows that drift and thin out.

The Citadel

  • The halls on floor 2 are furnished as the command's offices: a library with rows of bookshelves, offices with desks, a great hall, a lounge, a board room and a treasury.
  • The halls on floor 3 are a research lab: a building hall, a testing room, hazardous waste storage, a design office and a specimen room.
  • The hallways stay clear.
  • Every piece of furniture and equipment can be destroyed, and leaves a pile of rubble.

DOMINATOR

  • When DOMINATOR is beaten, the camera moves to it and it comes apart: it shudders, its core starts to glow, and fireballs tear it open. Control returns once the exposed core shows, and the meltdown countdown starts then.
  • The white flash stays reserved for the meltdown itself.
  • A grey bar is no longer drawn across DOMINATOR's north side.
  • Its music loops from the middle of the piece, where the full section returns, instead of going back to the opening.

Radiation

  • An irradiated enemy throws off bright green bits, one stream for each stack, so you can count the stacks at a glance.
  • A green cloud hangs over an irradiated enemy and thickens with each stack. It shows on every enemy and boss, including the ones drawn as 3D models.
  • A critical enemy no longer has a ring drawn round it. Its cloud pulses instead.
  • A meltdown's blast is a green fireball.
  • Fallout Tracks leaves two continuous lines of bright green liquid behind your tracks, with a moving net of light on them like the game's water.

Levels

  • Red Basin: the narrow gaps between its mesas are widened so the tank fits through all of them.

Music

  • The Iron Tundra has a new track, The Sky Remembers. Its old track is retired.
  • Dark Matter Canon plays on the after-action report after a won run.
  • Heroes Rise When Empires Fall plays during the core meltdown.

Sound

  • Two new explosion sounds. Each explosion now plays one of three at random.
  • A new launch sound wherever missiles are fired, yours included.
  • The Snow Dozer lets off steam as it takes aim and revs its engine as it charges. Its old engine roar is removed.
  • The menu button sound plays without the short delay it had.
