Patch Notes 3.2.2

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 26 November 2026

Highlights

  • Enemies find their way round walls, bosses and each other.
  • Tanks and other vehicles only drive forward or in reverse.
  • Every tank and boss now fires from the end of its guns.
  • DOMINATOR's shield is a dome of hexagonal panes that pushes everything out from under it.
  • The raised boxes under enemies are gone.

Enemy movement

  • Tanks and other vehicles only drive forward or in reverse. They used to slide sideways when stuck on each other.
  • A vehicle scraping a wall turns along it instead of pushing into it.
  • Enemies steer round bosses. They used to slide along a boss's side.
  • Enemies steer round DOMINATOR's dome while it's up.
  • Enemies no longer pick a spot under or behind a boss to drive to.
  • Enemies keep to the way they've chosen instead of turning back and forth.
  • A unit held between other units or against a boss squeezes free.
  • Units a boss sends out appear clear of its body. They used to appear inside it and get thrown out sideways.
  • A unit that lands inside a wall is set down at the nearest open spot. THE RESEARCHER's cars used to land inside walls.

DOMINATOR

  • Between phases its shield is a dome of hexagonal panes like Bulwark's. It was a placeholder.
  • The dome is stretched to fit DOMINATOR's shape.
  • As the dome rises it pushes your tank and every enemy out from under it.
  • Nothing on the ground can get back under the dome while it's up.
  • Your rounds now stop at the dome's wall.
  • The panes flare where your rounds hit.
  • A band of light sweeps the dome as its bar refills.
  • Its cannons, machine guns, autocannons and missile pods fire from their muzzles.
  • Its main gun's sweep fires from its two barrels in turn. It fired from the turret's side.
  • A flat disc no longer shows over its shut reactor.

Bosses

  • A hit now flashes the boss's own shape. It used to flash white boxes.
  • Stun and rage glows now follow the boss's shape.
  • BASTION waits 40% longer between attacks.
  • Boss guns fire from their muzzles. Most fired from a turret's middle or the air beside it.
  • THE ADMIRAL's machine gun turrets fire from each barrel in turn.
  • A flat bar and dot no longer show over THE ADMIRAL's tower.
  • BASTION's missiles leave its twelve tubes in turn.
  • BASTION has a railgun on its launcher. Its beam came from nothing.
  • BASTION's launcher turns with its railgun. The beam and the rails pointed different ways.
  • BASTION's drones rise off its launcher.
  • BASTION's tyres are round. From above they looked like flat black slabs.
  • BASTION's wheels are closed on their inner side. They looked hollow.
  • THE DREADNOUGHT's broadside comes from its battery's three guns.
  • THE FOUNDRY's four automated turrets are modelled on its deck. They fired from bare corners.
  • GEDDON's casemate guns fire from their muzzles.
  • GOLIATH's shells leave the ends of its barrels. They appeared well past them.
  • IRON HORSE's machine guns fire from each barrel in turn.
  • JUGGERNAUT's locomotive has a casemate gun each side. Its volley came out of its boiler.
  • THE LEVIATHAN's missiles leave its missile cells.
  • MIRAGE and its decoys fire missiles from their missile boxes.
  • MONOLITH's guns fire from their muzzles.
  • REAPER fires from its chin gun and its missile racks. Its rounds came out of the air beside it.
  • THE RESEARCHER's gun fires from the tip of its boom.

Guided rockets

  • The Fleet's guided rockets and missiles turn after you about half as sharply.
  • A guided rocket you get past now flies on straight instead of turning back.

Your tank

  • Rounds leave from the muzzle of each barrel. Some tanks fired them from inside the turret.
  • Twin guns fire from each barrel in turn.
  • The flamethrower's stream starts at its nozzle.
  • Barrel ends are round. They had square caps that stood out past the barrel.
  • Muzzles are dark inside. Some could be seen through.
  • The LONG GUN's extension is round like the rest of the barrel. It was a flat slab.
  • The MAMMOTH's ASSAULT HOWITZER is a 3D model. It was a flat picture laid over the turret.
  • With Avenger the MAMMOTH carries a 3D rotary cannon. It was a flat picture.
  • The SAPPER's PETARD is a 3D model.
  • The SKYGUARD's QUAD is a 3D model.

Enemy guns

  • Enemy tanks fire from the end of their barrels. Their rounds used to appear just ahead of the hull.
  • Tank destroyers, flak tracks, AT guns and security turrets fire from their barrels.
  • Flame tanks spray from their nozzles.
  • The armoured train's gun cars fire from their gun's muzzle. Their shells came out of the car's side.

Drawing

  • Flat sides no longer show under units drawn as 3D models.
  • The ground round a unit no longer rises with it.

The Citadel

  • Equipment crates, unstable reactors, decoys, tire tracks and scorch marks stay on their own floor. They used to show on other floors.
  • The boxes in the middle of the roof are gone.
  • Short wall stubs on the roof that looked like raised floor are now open floor.
  • The roof wall's top is painted right into its corners. A patch of bare floor showed beside each one.
