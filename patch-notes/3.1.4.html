Patch Notes 3.1.4

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 23 November 2026

Highlights

  • Altstadt is rebuilt around a street grid, with a grand cathedral, a fountain full of water and a railway station whose line runs off the map.
  • Altstadt's landmarks are held over three waves, and the enemy fights to take them back.
  • Your gun fires only at what you can see, and the camera pulls back so your whole range fits on screen. A LOCK button joins UNLOCK.
  • Autoloader, Hunter-Killer, Dozer Blade and Thermal Sights are toned down, evolutions take a little more building, and Contraband is worth taking.
  • The store is switched on for testing.

Altstadt

  • Three avenues and three cross streets run right through the town, so there are several ways north and no dead ends.
  • The three landmarks spread across the town, north to south and east to west.
  • The church is now a cathedral in the style of Notre-Dame: two flat-topped west towers, a long lead roof over the nave and choir, transept arms, lower aisles and a rounded east end. Flying buttresses arch from pinnacled piers up to its walls, and a tall spire at the crossing carries a gilt trident. Its churchyard is walled, with gates and rows of headstones.
  • The town square's fountain holds real water that ripples from its spout, under a statue of a sea god with his trident.
  • The station's track starts at a buffer stop on the platform, crosses the street and curves out of town to the south.
  • Each landmark is named at the start of wave 1, 4 or 7 and must be held to the end of wave 3, 6 or 9. If the enemy retakes it, that wave won't end until you take it back: a message tells you, and the arrow points the way. While you're away, the enemy now and then sends a squad to dig in on it.
  • Nights are about 13% lighter.
  • The town no longer leaves nooks a tank can get stuck in.
  • BASTION keeps to the streets, but when they don't bring it closer it drives straight at you, flattening rubble and houses on the way.

Combat and the view

  • The gun never fires at anything off screen or under the HUD. A boss counts once a good part of it is in view.
  • The camera draws back until your gun's whole reach fits on screen, further than before for long-range builds.
  • The button under the minimap is always there: LOCK locks the gun's current target, and UNLOCK releases it.
  • Headlights on every night level throw two longer beams with more light around the tank.
  • Enemy vehicles leave track marks under each track or wheel, as your tank does.

HUD

  • The threat dial's box is taller, with a bigger reading.
  • The top bar sits just below the camera cutout, giving the battlefield more room.
  • The shield and freeze timers show centred under the armor bar.

Balance

  • Autoloader: 15% faster reload a rank (was 20%).
  • Hunter-Killer: a kill takes the reload three quarters of the way. With Ready Rack it loads one round (was the whole rack).
  • Thermal Sights: +15% range (+25% at rank 2) and +10% damage.
  • Dozer Blade: still stops rounds head-on, but not rockets and missiles (Siege Plow still stops those). A ram hits once per impact, harder the faster you go.
  • Evolutions: one part at rank 3 and the other at rank 2. The other part comes up 1.5 times as often once you hold one (was twice).

Contraband

  • Experimental Overdrive overheats after 10 s of driving (was 7) and cools in 3 s (was 5).
  • Unstable Ammunition: +60% main-gun damage (was +40%), 10% duds (was 15%).
  • Priority Targeting System still lays the gun on the enemy with the most health. Its +40% damage applies to a target you've locked yourself.
  • Prototype Autoloader: 45% faster reloads (was 35%). 5% of volleys jam (was 8%), for 1.5 s (was 2.5).
  • Overcharged Capacitor: systems recharge 50% faster (was 40%). It overheats every 30 s (was 22) for 2 s (was 3).
  • Phoenix Protocol leaves your armor as it is when taken. The all-Composite armor starts only after it revives you.
  • Battlefield Scavenger Array: 3% crate chance (was 1.5%), with a 15% chance of a Rare. Reinforcements +20% (was +35%).
  • Experimental Warhead: +50% explosion damage (was +40%). Your own blasts only crack your armor. Enemy explosives have a 20% chance of a second segment (was 30%).
  • Directed Energy Conversion: cracks set it off as well as hits. It lasts 6 s (was 4) and adds 30% fire rate.
  • Ghost Signal Transmitter: hidden 5 s every 14 s (was 4 s every 16). The shelling only runs while you're hidden.
  • Fortress Mode deploys after 1 s still (was 1.5).
  • Unstable Reactor keeps a steady beat for most of its life, then races just before it blows. +50% damage in its field (was +30%), and its pulses only crack your armor.
  • Battlefield Reclaimer: 6% repair chance (was 4%).

Feats, Armory, leaderboard and Codex

  • Many feat and level-achievement targets are lowered to match how runs actually go.
  • The extra rerolls tile in the Armory counts Stage 1 and 2 clears, as it says.
  • Leaderboard rows show each player's current rank where it's known.
  • Codex progress counts entries that are partly unlocked.

The Citadel

  • All three terminals stand on each shop floor. One with nothing to offer says NO OFFERS.
  • Shadows behind the angled walls follow the wall's line, and nothing is hidden on the roof.
  • Decor against the walls is drawn in front of them.
  • Stray lines on the roof's walls are gone.
  • During the escape, the GO DOWN and STAIRS DOWN prompts no longer show.

Ruinous Facility

  • The floor is flat throughout.
  • The extraction smoke is red, as on every other level.
  • The REQ caches stand in their rooms, and their labels no longer overlap.
  • There is one hatch on the way in.

Every level

  • Text on the battlefield no longer bends the ground around it.
  • Only real hills bend with the camera's perspective. Built floors stay flat.
  • Thermal Sights no longer draws orange boxes over buildings at night.
  • Overhead beams no longer run across buildings.
  • BASTION's rocket launcher sits flush on its hull.

Store testing

  • The game's store is switched on for testing: the game starts as the free edition, and the full game can be unlocked with an in-app purchase.
