Patch Notes 3.2.6

Ironclad: Battle Tank for Android · 27 November 2026

Highlights

  • Line of sight costs far less to draw so the game runs smoother.
  • Houses on Black Coast, Ashen Foundry and Steel Corridor block your tank's view.

Performance

  • Enemies out of sight are faded as they're drawn. They used to be drawn into a separate full-screen picture twice a frame, which slowed phones down.

Line of sight

  • Houses on Black Coast, Ashen Foundry and Steel Corridor block your tank's view. The ground behind them used to stay lit.
