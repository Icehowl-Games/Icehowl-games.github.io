About

The Iron Fleet holds every front from the coast to the mountains. You have one tank.

Drop into a hostile front and take an upgrade after every wave. Turn one tank into a war machine the Iron Fleet never planned for, then fight your way into the Citadel to bring down their ultimate weapon. No two runs build the same machine.

  • 126 upgrades across four rarities. Stack the right ones and 92 synergies combine them into evolutions.
  • 14 bosses. Shoot off their weapons and find their weak points. The heaviest roll straight through buildings to reach you.
  • 10 battlefields with objectives to hold under fire, weather, battlefield events and hidden levels.
  • 6 vehicles that each change a rule. Swap the main gun for a Railgun, an Avenger rotary cannon or a Flamethrower.
  • Equipment crates on the battlefield call in fire missions, strafing runs and supply drops.
  • The Citadel: hack through the Iron Fleet's mountain fortress floor by floor, face DOMINATOR and escape as its core melts down.
  • Weekly operations put every player on the same run. Escalation raises the stakes for a higher score.
